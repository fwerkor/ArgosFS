# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

ArgosFS contributors, maintainers, and community participants are expected to
make collaboration respectful and constructive, regardless of experience,
background, identity, or level of involvement.

## Expected behavior

- Be courteous and assume good faith when discussing technical disagreements.
- Give actionable feedback about code, evidence, and ideas rather than people.
- Respect different experience levels and help newcomers find relevant context.
- Respect privacy, and avoid sharing someone else's private information.
- Accept constructive criticism and take responsibility for mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, personal attacks,
unwelcome sexual attention, sustained disruption, and publishing private
information without permission are not tolerated. This applies to issues,
pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project-related community spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **caoyuhang@fwerkor.com**. Include
relevant links and a brief description; do not post sensitive reports in public
issues. Maintainers will review reports, consider the safety and privacy of
those involved, and respond proportionately. Possible actions include a
request to change behavior, removal of offending content, temporary
restrictions, or a permanent ban from project spaces.

Project maintainers may moderate contributions and conversations to uphold
this policy. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible,
though disclosure may be necessary to investigate a concern or meet legal
obligations.
