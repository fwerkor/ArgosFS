Research & reproducibility

Follow the evidence.
Reproduce the run.

ArgosFS provides a testbed for placement, recovery, and storage automation. Experiment scripts retain raw records and summaries so behavior can be inspected.

Questions to investigate

What should a storage
controller earn your trust on?

Recovery

Does data survive faults?

Exercise interrupted operations, degraded reads, reconstruction, and consistency checks. Separate a successful recovery from an untested failure mode.

Placement

Does it adapt usefully?

Study workload shifts and uneven disks. Measure placement behavior and maintenance cost alongside foreground I/O.

Control

Can decisions be explained?

Compare manual and autopilot policies. Inspect selected actions, rejection reasons, and verification outcomes instead of relying on a single final score.
